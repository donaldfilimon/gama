# Gama engineering scope

The current product is the Zig/std-only graph in `build.zig`, governed by [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) and the [approved design](superpowers/specs/2026-10-03-zig-only-framework-design.md). Retain portable authoring/state/layout/drawing, cooperative plugins, TUI/plain, embedding, freestanding exports and textual MLIR. Production has no package dependencies or handwritten OS bindings. Current proof belongs only in [Capabilities](Capabilities.md); historical native product visions do not authorize new targets.
